# Kaya events log

A dated record of Kaya events, compiled 30 Oct 2026 from the Kaya Kreates and Twnda Facebook pages, the Tribe Hub Mw posts the owner shared, Google Drive poster folders and the Kaya Knowledge Pack.

Use it for throwback posts, past-event posts, philosophy posts and the "what we've built" story.
Internal client or pricing details (Knowledge Pack Part C) are deliberately left out.

**Status key**
- ✅ confirmed by posters or posts at the time
- ⚠️ inferred or conflicting: confirm before posting
- ❓ known to exist, details missing

**Page column** = what is on the Kaya Kreates Page now: *backdated* (campaign posted in the past), *2024 post* (original post), *none*.

### Ghetto Youth Festival 2 (2022)

- **When:** Wed 6 July 2022, 8am to 6pm, free entry.
- **Where:** Domasi College Ground ("Pa Carpet"), Domasi, Zomba.
- **By:** "The Tribe brings you…" (The Tribe / Tribe Hub Mw).
- **Partners on poster:** DOK Build Hardware, Sketch MW, The Tribe Hub, Redox Lab Films, Nkokomo Wall, Nkhakar Lodge.
- **Acts named:** Bee Jay, K Banton, Ace Jizzy, Dali Citty, Bouncy, Clovix, Nthondwa, Swsh, Dada Morer, Princess, Multi-key, Option, Keed F and more.
- **Activities:** football, netball, martial arts and a wheelchair race (visible in the photos).
- **Promo timeline (Tribe Hub):**
  - 29 Jun: announcement + programme poster
  - 5 Jul: "Mawa mubwera ku Domasi pa Carpet…"
  - 6 Jul: "Today" + match schedule posters
  - 8 Jul: "Zikomo / Thank you" poster
  - 10 Jul: crowd and wheelchair-race photos ("Si chisangalaro ichi?… #GYF22")
- **Contact on posters:** 0880 546 461.
- **Assets:** Drive "GHetto Youth Festival" (Keed F poster). The other photos come from Tribe Hub posts (fbids 123091513753447, 125168986879033, 125440330185232, 125440373518561, 126113403451258, 126735406722391, 126735430055722, 126735810055684, 126735846722347).

### Ghetto Youth Festival 3 (2023)

- **When:** Thu 6 July 2023, free entry.
- **Where:** Domasi College of Education, Zomba.
- **Powered by:** The Tribe Hub ("An event by The Tribe").
- **Partners:**
  - Castel Malawi: partner activation with the Bayala fun truck
  - Center for Collective Action: "Save one, save all" vaccination drive ("Tiyeni tikabaitse katemera")
  - National Bank of Malawi: account-opening stall ("Tiyeni tikatsegule ma account")
- **Programme:** martial arts and aerobics; the Ghetto Youth Challenge Cups (football, netball, basketball); live performances; arts and crafts. Stalls cost MK15,000 (+265 884 28 20 72). Hosts were George and Ginnah.
- **Musical acts (from the line-up poster):** Thomas Chibade, Gibo Lantos, Bee Jay, Ace Jizzy, Mtameni, Nthondwa, Bouncy, Trappy Beats, JEG, Clovix, DJ Smindloz, Postnegative, Dada Morer, Tribe Accapella, Option & Captain Cycle, K Millz, Droz, Tricker, Slyke Homek, Lipepedwa, Zexus, Comex, Princo, Marie, Yadah Mduf, Queen Tash, Besco, Roudizzy, Farai Willz, Adykiss, Nthambi, Hope Gee, Keed F, Chell D, Ngwazi, DJ Teknoh, Jembizo, Major Zack, Galizy, Waxt G, Asherbass, Yangtse, Ruth-Texta, Jay Fray, Shany Rapper, Camerieon MW, Mzeru Pdee, Kell Bon, Stewart, Oprisima, Yellow Buzy, General Chris, Rooney Dee, Omadizzy, Mekhey Oma, Tee Kay, Fives Vadoh.

- **Meet & Greet:** Fri 7 July 2023 from 5pm, Matsire Clinic Grounds, Songani. Bee Jay and Ace Jizzy, with DJ Smindloz and DJ Teknoh.
- **Promo timeline (Tribe Hub):**
  - 18 May: "save the date" poster
