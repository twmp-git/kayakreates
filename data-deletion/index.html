Data deletion

The Kreates Twnda Manager app holds no personal data about members of the public.

Remove the app from your Facebook account

Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → find "Kreates Twnda Manager" → Remove.

Ask us to delete your data

Email tawandampando@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the account the request is about. We will confirm and delete any information we hold about you within 30 days.
