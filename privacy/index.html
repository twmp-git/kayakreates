Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy covers the Kaya Kreates and Twnda Facebook Pages and "Kreates Twnda Manager", the Meta app we use to publish to them.

What the app does

Kreates Twnda Manager is used only by the Kaya Kreates team. It publishes our own posts to the Kaya Kreates and Twnda Facebook Pages, reads comments and basic statistics on those posts, replies to comments, and reads back each post's ID, link and time so we know what went out.

Information we collect

Only the Pages we manage (and the access tokens Meta issues for them), the content we publish ourselves, the IDs, links and times of those posts, and the comments and statistics Meta shows on them. We do not build profiles of, sell or keep personal information about people who visit, follow, like, comment on or share our posts; those interactions are covered by Meta's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and share nothing with third parties except Meta as needed to publish and manage our posts.

Storage

Post records are kept in our private working files. Access tokens are stored as protected secrets.

Children

Our Pages are for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

Any update will appear here with a new date. Questions: tawandampando@gmail.com
